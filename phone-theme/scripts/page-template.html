Android theme

Wallpapers, icons and widgets for your phone. Tap Save on anything you want.

1Wallpapers

Tap Save under a wallpaper.

Open it in your Gallery, tap ⋮, then Set as wallpaper. Pick lock screen or home screen.

"Lock" designs keep the logo low so the clock fits on top. "Home" designs keep it high so your apps fit below.

{{WALLPAPERS}}

2App icons

Pick green or ivory, then tap Save all icons. Open the file in My Files (Samsung) or Files (Google) and tap Extract.

Samsung: get Good Lock from the Galaxy Store, open Theme Park, then Icon, then Create new, and swap in the Bergins icons.

Any other phone: get Nova Launcher from the Play Store. Press and hold an app, tap Edit, tap the icon, then choose the matching picture.

Tap a single icon below to save just that one.

3Widgets

Save a widget picture, install any photo widget app from the Play Store, add its widget to your home screen and choose the picture. Use the wide ones at 4×2 and the square ones at 2×2.
